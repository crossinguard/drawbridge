---
title: "Canvas Assessment Review quick-start"
description: "Turn a Canvas course export into a bundle of files you can hand to a colleague or an assistant."
toolSlug: "assessment-review"
kind: "quick-start"
---

1. **Export the course from Canvas.** In the course, go to **Settings → Export Course Content** and choose
   **Common Cartridge (.imscc)**. A file with a `.zip` extension works too — it is the same archive.
2. **Open it here.** Drop the file onto the drop zone, or use **Choose an archive**. The archive itself is what the
   tool reads.
3. **Review.** The **Overview** tab says what the course holds and what the review found. The other tabs break it
   down: modules, assessments, quizzes, rubrics, the gradebook, and every finding with the values it compared. The
   **Review settings** card above the tabs decides what the review checks — the rule for when the instructions are
   silent, the retired terms, the words to look at, the term pattern, and how quizzes are grouped.
4. **Export.** Continue to the export step and download the bundle. It is a ZIP holding a short brief, the tables
   behind it, a detail file for each item that needs looking at, and a manifest that lists everything.

Upload `00_brief.md` first. It says what the course holds, what the review found, and what it could not settle, and
it points at the other files. The **Export settings** decide how much detail the bundle carries and which parts of
the course to leave out.

Nothing is uploaded. The export is read in your browser and forgotten when you close the tab.
