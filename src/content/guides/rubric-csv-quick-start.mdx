1. **Import the rubric.** Upload a .docx file, paste a table, or paste a previously exported Canvas CSV.
   - Drag and drop a Word document onto the dropzone.
   - Paste a table copied directly from Word, or as a markdown / tab-separated table.
   - If the document has multiple tables, select the correct one from the list.
2. **Review and edit.** Check that criteria and rating levels are correct. Name the rubric, choose a points preset, and adjust any individual rating points.
3. **Export.** Copy the CSV or download it, then import it into Canvas:
   - Open a Canvas course.
   - Go to **Rubrics** and click **+Rubric**.
   - Click **import** and choose the downloaded file.
