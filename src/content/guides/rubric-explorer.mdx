## What this tool helps you do

Rubric Explorer reads the rubrics in a Canvas course export. You can read criteria and rating levels, see which activities attach each rubric, find matching content, and compare two rubrics side by side.

It only describes what is in the export. It does not decide whether a rubric is good, flag problems, change Canvas, or create a download.

## What you need

You need a Canvas **Common Cartridge** course export with an **.imscc** filename. Renamed **.zip** archives are not accepted. Canvas's QTI-only and files-only ZIP exports are different formats and are not supported.

Canvas can include selected content in an export. Rubric Explorer can only show the rubrics and activities in your file; it cannot tell whether the export was selective or complete.

## Open a course

1. Select **Choose a course export**, or drop the file onto the import area.
2. Wait while Drawbridge reads the file.
3. Review the rubric overview and choose a rubric.

If you already opened the course in Course Explorer, open Rubric Explorer without importing again. The two tools share the course while you navigate within Drawbridge.

## Browse and read a rubric

1. Use **Search rubrics** to find words in a title, criterion, rating, or linked activity.
2. Use **Relationship** to narrow the list to rubrics with linked activities, no linked activities, exact-content copies, or same-name variations.
3. Select a rubric to open its details.
4. Read its criteria and rating levels. Long descriptions appear beneath the relevant criterion or rating.

The detail view includes the rubric's stated points, the sum of criterion points when every criterion has a value, and other settings recorded in the export. These are facts about the rubric, not warnings.

## Understand linked activities

A linked activity is an assignment, quiz, or graded discussion in the export that attaches the rubric. The link shows whether the rubric is **Used for grading** or **Attached for reference**.

Select an activity to open it in Course Explorer. The course remains open during this handoff.

**No linked activities in this export** does not prove that the rubric is unused in Canvas. The activities may not have been included in a selective export, and rubrics are not module items themselves.

## Explore content groups

**Exact-content copies** have matching criteria, ratings, long descriptions, and points. Their titles and Canvas-generated identifiers may differ.

**Same-name, different content** groups contain rubrics with the same normalized title but different content versions. Select any rubric in a group to read it, or use **Compare** to examine two versions.

These groups help you navigate related rubrics. They are not quality judgments.

## Compare two rubrics

1. Select **Compare**.
2. Choose a **Left rubric** and a **Right rubric**.
3. Review the summary and each criterion position.

Criteria are compared in their exported order. **Same** means the complete criterion matches. **Different** means something in that criterion or its ratings differs. **Only in left** or **Only in right** means one rubric has a criterion at that position and the other does not.

Open **Show rating levels** within a compared criterion to read its ratings. Points and long descriptions are included in the comparison.

## Session and privacy

The export is processed entirely in your browser. It is not uploaded, saved to an account, or written to browser storage. Moving between Course Explorer, Rubric Explorer, and their guides keeps the current course session.

A browser refresh, a new tab, closing the tab, or **Close course** forgets the imported course. After a refresh, choose the export again.

## Troubleshooting

### No rubrics appear

The original course may not contain rubrics, or rubrics may not have been selected when Canvas created the export. Create a fresh Common Cartridge export and make sure rubrics are included.

### An activity or rubric link is missing

Drawbridge can only follow relationships included in the file. A selective export may contain a rubric without its activities, or an activity without its referenced rubric.

### Two rubrics look identical but have different comparison headings

Titles and some Canvas settings are separate from rubric content. The comparison summary explains whether criteria, ratings, long descriptions, and points match even when titles differ.
